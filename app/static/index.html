Public-welfare infrastructure · bounded autonomous agent

Turn verified feed risk into accountable intervention.

AFRN detects projected dog/cat feed gaps, searches only rule-eligible supply, proposes a match and keeps unresolved needs visible. Humans retain authority over eligibility, diet safety, dispatch, incidents and case closure.

checking ledger…

The agent may

Observe · forecast · open a need case · propose an eligible match · escalate an unresolved gap.

The agent may not

Approve a recipient · approve a diet · dispatch feed · close an incident · declare a case resolved without evidence.

v0 launch scope

Verified dog/cat organizations, sealed commercial complete feed, append-only audit history, human-reviewed proposals, and one goal: prove at least one preventable stockout can be resolved safely and traceably.

This is coordination software, not veterinary advice or a legal authorization system. Swagger API: /docs
