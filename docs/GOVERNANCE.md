# Governance and safety boundary

## v0 scope

- Dogs and cats only.
- Verified recipient organizations only.
- Sealed, unopened commercial complete feed only on the autonomous proposal path.
- No exotic wildlife.
- No medical/therapeutic diet decisions.
- No prepared-food or ingredient transformation without a separately approved local protocol and named professional authority.

## Binary gates, not optimization weights

Safety, legality, recipient status, batch identity, species compatibility, storage feasibility and required professional approval are hard gates. A high urgency score can never override a failed gate.

## Human-only authority

Named humans retain authority over:

1. recipient eligibility and suspension;
2. veterinary/nutrition approval;
3. food-safety exceptions and local legal interpretation;
4. final match approval and physical dispatch;
5. incident response and recall;
6. case resolution.

The public-welfare agent may increase attention or assistance. It may not autonomously deny aid eligibility.

## Privacy

Do not store private donor/beneficiary data in public Nocturnal exports. The v0 public dashboard exposes aggregate operational state only. Real deployments should use pseudonymous IDs and a partner-controlled identity store.

## Stop conditions

Pause live operation if any serious food-safety incident occurs, the audit chain cannot be verified, the local rule pack loses professional approval, recipient inventory becomes systematically untrustworthy, or transport repeatedly costs more than direct purchase without a distinct welfare benefit.
