# Pilot gate

The software is not the charity. A live pilot exists only when an operational partner, professional authority and real physical workflow exist.

## Sequence

1. **Synthetic / demo** — prove state transitions, unsafe rejection, ledger integrity and non-autonomous approval.
2. **Shadow mode** — import real inventory/supply data; the partner continues normal operations while AFRN independently forecasts and proposes.
3. **Assisted mode** — humans review AFRN proposals and may execute them through the existing workflow.
4. **Bounded live pilot** — two or three verified dog/cat recipients, approximately 100–300 animals, limited feed classes, named veterinary/nutrition authority and logistics owner.

## Minimum credible success

- 100% traceable accepted batches.
- 0 serious food-safety incidents.
- >85% on-time pickups when AFRN is used for dispatch coordination.
- >80% scheduled recipient inventory updates.
- At least one independently documentable stockout prevented.
- At least two organizations willing to continue.
- Measure verified animal-days, not app registrations or gross kilograms collected.

## Primary impact metric

**Preventable unresolved feed gap:** animal-days during which a verified feeding need remained uncovered even though a feasible intervention existed.

Secondary metrics include stockouts prevented, verified animal-days protected, procurement cost displaced, handling/spoilage loss, time-to-resolution, human overrides, rejected unsafe batches, audit exceptions and partner retention.
