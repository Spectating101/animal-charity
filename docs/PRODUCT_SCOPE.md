# Product scope

## Validation objective

Demonstrate a complete synthetic loop:

1. represent recipient demand and depletion;
2. submit a traceable supply batch;
3. apply deterministic hard gates;
4. require named manual approval;
5. generate ordered operational tasks;
6. collect synthetic custody and feeding evidence;
7. calculate an auditable impact claim;
8. suspend a batch and all derived matches during an incident drill.

## Included in V0

- one synthetic recipient and animal group;
- commercial-feed batch intake;
- hard rejection rules;
- human-readable rule trace;
- match score only after hard gates pass;
- manual approval;
- task dependencies;
- evidence-gated impact calculation;
- incident suspension;
- append-only audit events;
- browser dashboard and JSON API.

## Explicitly excluded

- real food or diet approval;
- homemade diet formulation;
- veterinary or legal authorization;
- live partner authentication;
- payments, donor tax receipts or fundraising;
- route optimization;
- mobile applications;
- wildlife and medically complex populations.
