# Safety boundaries

This repository is a validation product, not veterinary, legal or food-safety authorization.

## Non-negotiable controls

- Real-world batches fail the `MODE-001` hard gate.
- Safety rules are code and data, not hidden only in prompts.
- Hard failures cannot be offset by a match score.
- Every eligible match still requires manual approval.
- Anonymous leftovers, recalled goods, raw meat, cooked bones, mould, chemical exposure and unknown temperature history are rejected.
- Impact remains blocked until pickup, delivery and feeding-evidence tasks are complete.
- A batch suspension also suspends every derived match.
- Every operational mutation creates an audit event.

## Before any live pilot

A named local veterinary/nutrition authority, legal review, recipient verification, logistics ownership and incident process are required. The sample rules in this repository are intentionally conservative and synthetic.
